# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly and fairly.

To report an incident:

- Prefer private channels (e.g., GitHub Security Advisories for sensitive issues; see SECURITY.md).
- Otherwise, contact the maintainers via GitHub Issues with a clear description.

Maintainers will respect confidentiality and act to preserve a safe, welcoming environment.

For full details, see the official Contributor Covenant 2.1 linked above.
